Service Desk & Help Desk Procedures

Systematic triage, response targets, and technical execution rules to safeguard against logistical export delays.

Incident Logging Channels

Workers experiencing technical issues with hardware terminals, local printers, network connections, or enterprise applications must report the problem immediately via one of the following methods:

  • Email Intake: Submit a detailed description to the shared corporate helpdesk inbox.
  • Internal IT Portal: Open an active technical assistance ticket directly on the internal dashboard.

Impact-Driven Triage & SLA Matrix

Frontline helpdesk personnel must immediately classify incoming support issues into four distinct severity tiers based on real-time operational interruption data:

Severity Tier Impact Description Initial Response Target Target Resolution Window
Critical Outage Core branch gateway offline or central database server crash halting operations across entire sites. Within 15 minutes Ongoing engineering work until resolved
High Severity Localized system errors, such as a branch-wide network printer failure preventing an entire team's output. Within 1 hour Within 4 hours
Medium Severity Isolated single-user problems, such as software lockouts from Sage ERP Payroll or Microsoft Office 365. Within 4 hours Within 24 hours
Low Severity Requests for basic configurations, small aesthetic software issues, or non-blocking queries. Within 24 hours Within 3 business days

Remote & Operational Rules

  • Remote Assistance: Technicians at the Cape Town head office will use secure, encrypted remote-control applications to fix configurations for regional branches and field sales teams.
  • Root-Cause Documentation: Before a ticket can be closed, the responding technician must explicitly record the verified root cause and specific technical actions performed.

Escalation & Validation

  • Hierarchical Pathway: Issues unresolved by frontline support within 2 hours of continuous troubleshooting must be formally escalated to the Senior Systems Administrator or IT Manager.
  • User Validation Loops: When an incident file is closed, the impacted employee is automatically notified to verify the resolution and provide helpdesk quality feedback.
