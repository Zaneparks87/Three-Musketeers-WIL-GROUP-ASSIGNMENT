Service Desk & Help Desk Procedures

Systematic triage, response targets, and technical execution rules to safeguard against logistical export delays.

Incident Logging Channels

Workers experiencing technical issues with hardware terminals, local printers, network connections, or enterprise applications must report the problem immediately via one of the following methods:

  • Email Intake: Submit a detailed description to the shared corporate helpdesk inbox.
  • Internal IT Portal: Open an active technical assistance ticket directly on the internal dashboard.

Impact-Driven Triage & SLA Matrix

Frontline helpdesk staff are required to quickly categorize incoming support issues into four separate severity tiers using real-time operational interruption data:

Severity Tier Impact Description Initial Response Target Target Resolution Window
Critical Outage Events that stop business operations across whole sites,such as a central Cape Town database server breaking or a main branch gateway going offline. Within 15 minutes Ongoing engineering work until resolved
High Severity Localized system issues that limits a team's production like a branch-wide shared network printer failure. Within 1 hour Within 4 hours
Medium Severity Isolated single-user issues that limit individual output such a salesperson locked out of Microsoft Office 365 or Sage ERP Payroll. Within 4 hours Within 24 hours
Low Severity Simple information requests, surface level software defects or non-blocking queries. Within 24 hours Within 3 business days

Remote & Operational Rules

  • Remote Assistance:To help users at the Durban branch, remote growth users and field sales teams, technicians at the Cape Town headquarters will use secure encrypted remote control applications to troubleshoot and patch terminal configurations over the network.
  • Root-Cause Documentation:Before a support ticket may be officially closed and recorded as resolved in the database, the responding technician must properly document the fault's verified root cause and the specific technical activities taken to correct it.

Escalation & Validation

  • Hierarchical Pathway: If a frontline support technician cannot successfully resolve or patch an ongoing technical issue within two hours of continuous troubleshooting an incident ticket must be formally raised to the Senior Systems Administrator or the IT Manager for advanced structural assistance.
  • User Validation Loops:The affected employee receives an automatic electronic notification when an incident file is closed requesting them to confirm that the issue has been fixed while collecting feedback to track helpdesk quality indicators.
