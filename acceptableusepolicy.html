Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Core guidelines regulating the usage of corporate devices, organizational networks, and Microsoft 365 services.

Permitted System Use

  • Utilize official business infrastructure primarily for authorized corporate operations and communications.
  • Ensure restricted personal use does not violate laws, compromise your tasks, or consume heavy bandwidth channels.
  • Always treat clients and internal team members with professional digital courtesy and respect.
  • Store work documentation exclusively inside verified, shared organizational directories.
  • Maintain strict physical monitoring and safety of company computers during multi-site travel.
  • Proactively report accidental asset data exposures immediately to IT support teams.

Strictly Prohibited Actions

  • Do not configure unapproved browser plug-ins, alter web filters, or navigate to dangerous external destinations.
  • Never distribute copyrighted materials or operate pirated application builds inside company machines.
  • Do not connect private external hardware routers, network access points, or unvetted storage drives.
  • Never transfer confidential organizational data sets to personal chat lines or private emails.
  • Do not intentionally obscure system security oversights or data handling slip-ups from IT managers.
  • System monitoring tools may legally review corporate networks at any time; expect zero total privacy for unauthorized streams.
