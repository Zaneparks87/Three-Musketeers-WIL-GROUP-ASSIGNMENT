Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Core guidelines regulating the usage of corporate devices, organizational networks, and Microsoft 365 services.

Permitted System Use

  • For authorized communication and legitimate work tasks,use business systems.
  • Restricted personal use must not violate any laws or policies, interface with obligations or use excessive bandwidth.
  • in digital communications,show respect to both clients and coworkers.
  • Keep work related papers in authorized shared spaces with the necessary authorization.
  • When moving between locations,keep devices physically safe.
  • Instead of attempting to hide accidental data sharing,report it right away.
  • AFC may keep an eye on corporate system in a way which is lawful, open and reasonable.

Strictly Prohibited Actions

  • Avoid installing unaproved browser extensions, bypassing web filtering and visiting harmful websites.
  • Never distribute copyrighted files without authorization or download software that has been copied.
  • Without IT permission, do not connect your own routers, access points or storage devices.
  • Don't send business papers to personal chat apps or private email addresses.
  • Never transfer confidential organizational data sets to personal chat lines or private emails.
  • Do not intentionally hide system security oversights or data handling mistakes from IT managers.
  • System monitoring tools may legally review corporate networks at any time; expect zero total privacy for unauthorized streams.
