IT Security Policy Guidelines

Purpose: To safeguard AFC's computers, network, data, and online services against unauthorized access and cyber threats.

Access Control & User Accounts

  • Unique Accounts: Each employee must have a dedicated account with a strong, unique password.
  • MFA Mandate: Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for administrator accounts, remote access, and email systems.
  • Least Privilege: Employees may only access the specific information required to perform their jobs.
  • Termination Protocol: System access must be immediately revoked when an employee leaves or changes positions.

Network & Wireless Perimeter Security

  • Site-to-Site VPN: All regional branches must connect to the Cape Town HQ via secure, encrypted VPN tunnels.
  • Firewall Inspection: Inbound and outbound perimeter traffic must be continuously filtered by firewalls.
  • VLAN Segmentation: Separate VLAN networks must isolate departments, servers, and guest Wi-Fi streams.
  • Guest Isolation: Visitor Wi-Fi channels must strictly prevent access to company servers and private devices.

Data Protection, Privacy & POPIA Compliance

  • POPIA Alignment: All collection and handling of client or finical data must strictly adhere to the South African Protection of Personal Information Act.
  • Storage Bans: Unauthorized cloud accounts, personal emails, or unknown USB drives must never host corporate files.
  • Website Security: Only authorized administrators using MFA are permitted to publish web platform updates.

Business Continuity, Backups & Incidents

  • Immutable Backups: Critical databases and Sage ERP data must maintain at least one completely offline, ransomware-protected backup.
  • Physical Security: Server rooms and network utility cabinets must remain locked and limited to authorized technicians.
  • Immediate Reporting: Employees must instantly report suspected hacks, strange account changes, or lost devices to the IT Manager.
