IT Security Policy Guidelines

Purpose: To safeguard AFC's computers, network, data, and online services against unauthorized access and cyber threats.

Access Control & User Accounts

  • Unique Accounts:Every employee needs their own account with a secure password.
  • MFA Mandate: Multi-factor authentication is required for administrator accounts, remote access and email systems.
  • Least Privilege:Permissions granted to users are limited to the data necessary to perform their specific tasks.
  • Termination Protocol:When an employee is removed or their position changes, system access must be immediately terminated.

Network & Wireless Security

  • Site-to-Site VPN:Secure site-to-site VPN tunnels are required for all regional branches to link to the Cape Town headquarters.
  • Firewall Inspection:To stop illegal links, perimeter firewalls must examine incoming and outgoing data packets.
  • VLAN Segmentation:VLANs are required for internal departments, servers, office printers, staff devices and guest Wi-Fi.
  • Guest Isolation:Guest Wi-Fi access channels must remain totally isolated from the main corporate business network. Visitor devices must not be able to see company servers, local printers or private database assets

Data Protection, Privacy & POPIA Compliance

  • POPIA Alignment: The POPIA's statutory requirements must be strictly followed to by all corporate information pipelines.
  • Storage Bans: Unauthorized cloud drives, personal emails, and unidentified USB disks are prohibited for employees.
  • Website Security: It is not allowed for employees to connect private devices, install unapproved external programs or share passwords.

Business Continuity, Backups & Incidents

  • Immutable Backups:System backups must be kept entirely offline and safe from ransomware attacks in at least one copy.
  • Physical Security: Server rooms and network utility cabinets must remain locked and limited to authorized technicians.
  • Immediate Reporting: Employees must instantly report suspected hacks, strange account changes, or lost devices to the IT Manager.
