Acquisition Procedures & Approvals

Enforcing complete device configuration consistency and rigid budget controls across regional locations.

The IT Asset Procurement Workflow

All software additions, terminal hardware perimeter purchases, and workstation deployments follow this rigid process:

  • 1

    Formal Request Origin

    Branch supervisors or department leads must formally submit hardware/license requirements through the internal IT port registry tool.

  • 2

    Technical Compliance Audit

    The Cape Town IT Manager manually checks the file to ensure the systems completely support Windows 11 and match office printing configurations.

  • 3

    Vetted Supplier Restrictions

    Purchases must exclusively originate from certified, pre-approved South African tech vendors to protect active long-term enterprise warranty tracks.

  • 4

    Financial Authorization Threshold Tiers

    Under R15,000: Requires the digital signature of the IT Manager.
    Over R15,000: Undergoes strict dual validation from both the Chief Accountant and IT Manager in Cape Town before a purchase order triggers.

  • 5

    Asset Management Mapping

    Upon branch delivery, technicians must instantly log the system's tracking number, MAC address, and serial keys into the central database.

  • 6

    Standardized Device Staging

    Workstations must be clean flashed with the official software baseline configuration image (Windows 11, Office 365, Sage ERP, corporate security tools) before deployment to any user.

  • 7

    Certified Data Destruction

    Retired or faulty media components must undergo formal data wiping procedures to safely and totally erase proprietary corporate and customer histories.
